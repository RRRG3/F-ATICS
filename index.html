    races × sims per race
    Read these numbers carefully. This samples a “ground truth” race from the model’s own distribution, then scores the model against it. It therefore measures internal consistency — whether the sampler and the stated probabilities agree — and not whether the model predicts real races. That is why Top-5 sits at 100%: it is true by construction. For genuine accuracy see the walking-forward backtest above, which scores against real historical results the model never saw.
