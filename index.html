🏁 F1 Basics & Rules

Everything you need to know to get started with Formula 1

🏎️

What is Formula 1?

Formula 1 is the highest class of international racing for open-wheel single-seater formula racing cars. It's the pinnacle of motorsport featuring the fastest cars and best drivers in the world.

🏆

Championship System

Drivers earn points based on finishing position (25 for 1st, 18 for 2nd, 15 for 3rd, etc.). The driver with the most points at season end wins the World Championship.

⏱️

Race Weekend Format

Friday: Practice sessions
Saturday: Final practice & Qualifying
Sunday: Race day (usually 305km or 2 hours)

🎯

Qualifying

Three knockout sessions (Q1, Q2, Q3) determine starting grid positions. Fastest lap in Q3 gets pole position. Bottom 5 eliminated in Q1 and Q2.

🔧

Pit Stops

Teams must use at least 2 different tire compounds during the race. Pit stops take ~2-3 seconds for tire changes. Strategy is crucial for winning.

🚩

Flags & Penalties

Yellow: Danger, slow down
Red: Session stopped
Blue: Let faster car pass
Checkered: Race finished

⚡

DRS (Drag Reduction System)

Rear wing opens in designated zones when within 1 second of car ahead. Provides ~10-12 km/h speed boost for overtaking opportunities.

🏁

Points System

1st: 25pts | 2nd: 18pts | 3rd: 15pts | 4th: 12pts | 5th: 10pts | 6th: 8pts | 7th: 6pts | 8th: 4pts | 9th: 2pts | 10th: 1pt
Fastest Lap: +1pt (if in top 10)
