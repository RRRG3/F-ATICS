What is Formula 1?

Formula 1 is the highest class of international racing for open-wheel single-seater formula racing cars. It's the pinnacle of motorsport featuring the fastest cars and best drivers in the world.

🏆

Championship

Drivers earn points based on finishing position. The driver with the most points at season end wins the World Championship.

⏱️

Race Weekend

Friday: Practice
Saturday: Qualifying
Sunday: Race

🎯

Qualifying

Three knockout sessions (Q1, Q2, Q3) determine starting grid positions. Fastest lap in Q3 gets pole position.

🔧

Pit Stops

Teams must use at least 2 different tire compounds. Pit stops take ~2-3 seconds. Strategy is crucial.

⚡

DRS

Rear wing opens in zones when within 1s of car ahead. Provides speed boost for overtaking.
